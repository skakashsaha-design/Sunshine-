# 18. "WHAT DOES THIS MEAN?" SYSTEM

Every technical metric gets an information button.

Explain simply:

Temperature  
= How hot or cold the air is.

Feels-like  
= How the temperature may feel after considering factors such as humidity and wind.

Dew point  
= The temperature at which air becomes saturated with moisture.

Humidity  
= The amount of water vapor in the air relative to the air's capacity at that temperature.

Pressure  
= The force exerted by the atmosphere.

Wind  
= Sustained movement of air.

Gust  
= A short-duration increase in wind speed.

Wind direction  
= The direction FROM which the wind is coming.

Visibility  
= Approximate distance at which objects can be clearly seen.

Cloud cover  
= The portion of the sky covered by clouds.

Precipitation  
= Water falling from the atmosphere, including rain and snow.

Rain probability  
= Probability of measurable precipitation at the selected location during the stated period.

UV index  
= Measure of ultraviolet radiation reaching the surface.

AQI  
= Index describing the potential health impact of air pollution.

PM2.5  
= Very small airborne particles 2.5 micrometers or smaller.

CAPE  
= Atmospheric energy that can support strong upward convection and thunderstorms.

Provide Bengali explanations when Bengali is selected.
